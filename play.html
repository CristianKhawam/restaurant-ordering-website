Burger Maze Challenge

Guide the burger bun to the red exit. Make it out before you run out of moves or time and unlock up to 20% off your order — bigger mazes, bigger discounts!

Game settings

Whole number from 5 (5×5) to 15 (15×15).

How to play

  • Use the arrow keys (or W A S D).
  • Or click a square next to the bun.
  • On a phone, use the arrow pad under the maze.
  • The clock starts as soon as the maze appears. Run out of moves or time and you have to start over!
